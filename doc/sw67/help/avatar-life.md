# Avatar Life stats, levels and careers

UNAVAILABLE. The Avatar Life system (mana, wellness, energy, hygiene, mood,
reputation, dedication, social, levels, XP, skills, careers, achievements,
activities and cooldowns) is not built yet, and there is no records server.

What this means today:

- King Jahmir cannot read anyone's stats, level, XP or activity history.
- He cannot say why a stat did or did not change, because no records exist.
- No level requirements have been defined, so he cannot list them.

## Why didn't my shower (or another activity) change my stats?

There are no Avatar Life records yet, so nobody can check why an activity
did or did not change a stat. When the records service exists, King Jahmir
will check your own activity records for three things: whether the product
was approved, whether the session completed, and whether a cooldown applied.
He will never decide from the conversation alone that an activity happened.

## How it will work once built

Design rules, already decided:

- Stats and XP change only from activity sessions the server records and
  calculates. A chat message, a claim, or a script alone never changes them.
- Rewards come only from approved products (see the creator connectors help
  page).
- King Jahmir will read only your own records, only when you turn on
  **Live account help**, and will explain results from those records.
