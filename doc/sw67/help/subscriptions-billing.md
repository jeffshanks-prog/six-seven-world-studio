# Subscriptions and billing

- Proposed plans: **Basic** $19.99/month, **DJ & Hosting** $29.99/month,
  **AI Creator** add-on $14.99/month, credit packs from $5. Prices can still
  change and no trial length is set.
- Billing is PLANNED (Milestone 5). There is no billing service, so no
  charge is possible and no subscription can be bought yet. Every feature is
  open in development builds.
- Your projects, photos, profiles and backups are your own files and stay
  usable without a subscription. Accessibility is never gated.
- There is no 6|7 account service yet. Your Second Life password goes only
  to Linden Lab.
- For billing questions, ask King Jahmir to prepare a support request.
