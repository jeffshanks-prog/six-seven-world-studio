# Getting support

1. Ask King Jahmir to "prepare a support request", or describe the problem.
2. He drafts a summary and shows it to you.
3. Check it, then click **Confirm** to send it, or **Cancel**.

Requests go to the 6|7 owner's review queue on the King Jahmir server. No
email or ticket desk is connected yet, so there is no promised reply time.
Do not include passwords. This software is not provided or supported by
Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life; Second Life account problems go to
Linden Lab support.
