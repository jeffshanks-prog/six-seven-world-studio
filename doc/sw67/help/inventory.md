# Inventory

- **Me > Inventory...** opens the standard inventory window: folders,
  search, filters, and the Recent tab.
- The 6|7 **Inventory Organizer** is PLANNED.
- King Jahmir does not read your inventory contents. Describe what you see
  and he can suggest how to organise it.
