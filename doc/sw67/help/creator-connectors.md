# Creator connectors and product approval

UNAVAILABLE. Product registration, the connector script and the approval
server are not built yet. These rules are decided and will apply:

- Jeff, the 6|7 owner, controls which creators and products are approved.
- Having a connector script in your furniture does not by itself authorise
  any stat reward.
- The server validates registered products and each activity session.
- Only approved capabilities and effects the server calculates are allowed.
- No product is approved because of a chat message, including one to King
  Jahmir. He cannot approve anything.

If you are building furniture now: build and test it as a normal Second Life
product (Creator Projects helps keep the files together). When registration
