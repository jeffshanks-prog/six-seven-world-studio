# Inventory

- **Me > Inventory...** opens the standard inventory window: folders,
  search, filters, and the Recent tab.
- **Me > 6|7 Studio > Inventory Cleanup Preview...** suggests moving loose
  items into their system folders and shows likely duplicates for you to
  review. It shows exactly what would move, moves only what you confirm,
  and **Undo** puts it back. It never deletes anything.
- King Jahmir does not read your inventory contents. Describe what you see
  and he can suggest how to organise it.
