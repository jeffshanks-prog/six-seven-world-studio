# Viewer settings

- **Me > Preferences...** holds the viewer's settings (graphics, sound,
  chat, privacy, controls).
- **World > Improve graphics speed...** is the standard graphics helper.
- **Me > 6|7 Studio > Performance Assistant...** explains your measurements
  and offers presets you can preview and undo. See the Performance Assistant
  help page.
- Settings Backup and Restore is PLANNED.
