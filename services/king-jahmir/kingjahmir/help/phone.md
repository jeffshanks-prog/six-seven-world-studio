# 6|7 Phone Companion

A web page for your phone with an event's RSVPs, a calendar reminder, its
shot list and a private team chat. It never asks for your Second Life
password, and it is not a Second Life viewer: use the viewer to be in-world.

**Set it up (host):**

1. In the viewer, open **Me > 6|7 Studio > Event Control Room...**, pick an
   event and press **Send to phone**. You need to be signed in to 6|7 in
   King Jahmir's Settings tab.
2. Press **Pair a phone**. The viewer shows a code like ABCD-2345 and the
   web address to open. The code works once, for 10 minutes.
3. On your phone, open that address and type the code.

**On the phone:**

- **Reminder**: **Add to calendar** puts the event in your phone's
  calendar with an alert 15 minutes before.
- **Shot list**: tick shots off; the host's viewer keeps the list itself.
- **Team chat**: only the host and the team members they invited see it.
  Messages are deleted after the gateway's retention period.
- **Team**: the host makes a one-use invite link for each team member and
  sends it privately. **Remove** stops that phone opening the event.
- **RSVPs**: closed until the host presses **Open RSVPs**. Guests answer
  with their name, profile link or account ID, and whether they're coming.
  Announcements are only for guests who tick that box themselves. Answers
  aren't verified. In the viewer, **Get RSVPs from phone** in Event Control
  Room adds them to the event's guest list in Party Contacts.

Push notifications are UNAVAILABLE: the page has no app to notify you
with, so use the calendar reminder.
