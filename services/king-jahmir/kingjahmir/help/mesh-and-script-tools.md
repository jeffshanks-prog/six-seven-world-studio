# Mesh and script tools

- **Script Studio** (from Alchemy): **Build > Script Studio**. An editor for
  LSL and SLua with completion, problems, formatting and the LSL optimizer.
- **Mesh upload**: the standard Second Life mesh uploader (**Build > Upload > Model...**).
  Upload costs are charged by Second Life, not by 6|7.
- **Creator Projects** keeps your mesh sources, textures and scripts
  together (see that help page).
- AI Mesh, Script and Texture Studios are PLANNED (Milestone 3).
