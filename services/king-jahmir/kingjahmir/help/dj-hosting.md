# DJ and hosting tools

AVAILABLE now, from **Me > 6|7 Studio**:

- **Event Control Room...**: your event schedule with countdowns, team
  roles, cue cards, a stream check, and announcement drafts. A draft is
  sent only after you approve its exact text and confirm the send.
  From here you can also pair the **6|7 Phone Companion**, send an event
  to it and get its RSVPs (see the 6|7 Phone help page).
- **Party Contacts...**: your friends, your party contacts and event guest
  lists, kept by account ID. Save someone from the People lists'
  right-click menu (**Save to Party Contacts**). It never sends a friend
  request or removes a friend. Invitations are previewed, you can leave
  anyone out, and a send asks first and goes out at most 40 at a time.
- **Sound Check...**: a 3-band monitor EQ, input gain, meters and a
  clipping warning. It is Monitor Only: your broadcast app sends your
  stream, and 6|7 doesn't change it.
- **Event Rehearsal...**: run through an event without sending, broadcasting
  or paying anything.
- The **DJ** and **Host** layouts in **Workspace Profiles**, and the
  **Event** preset in the **Performance Assistant**.
