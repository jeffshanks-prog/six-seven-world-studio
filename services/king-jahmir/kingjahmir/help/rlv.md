# RLV / RLVa

6|7 World Studio includes RLVa 2.5, the RestrainedLove extensions some
scripted objects use. It is **off by default**.

- To turn it on, enable RLVa in **Me > Preferences...**. RLVa starts at
  your next login. It is always your choice; nothing turns it on for you.
- **Me > 6|7 Studio > Accessibility...** shows whether RLV is off, on but
  not yet active, or active with a number of restrictions, and has **Show
  active restrictions**.
- When it is on, the **RLVa** menu appears; **RLVa > Restrictions...** lists
  what objects are currently restricting.
- 6|7 tools check RLV restrictions before acting. For example Product
  Photography will not move your camera while an object holds a camera
  restriction, and says which restriction stopped it.
- King Jahmir cannot change RLV settings and will never help get around a
  restriction or anyone's consent. Changing RLV settings is something you do
  yourself in Preferences.
