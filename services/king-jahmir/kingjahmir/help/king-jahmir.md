# King Jahmir

King Jahmir is an AI assistant. He answers questions about 6|7 World Studio
from these help pages and the feature registry, asks follow-up questions, and
can open app windows for you. He can be wrong; check important answers.

## Opening him

- Click the gold crown bubble. It sits at the right edge of the screen, above
  the bottom toolbar, and you can drag it anywhere.
- Or use **Me > 6|7 Studio > King Jahmir**.
- The bubble shows a count when he has replied while the panel was closed.
  The count is written as text ("2 new"), not shown by colour alone.

## The conversation panel

- Type in the message box and press **Enter** or click **Send**.
- Starter buttons: **Explain my stats**, **How do I use this feature?**,
  **Help me build furniture**, **Show my next level requirements**,
  **Troubleshoot a problem**, **What can I do here?**
- **A-** and **A+** change the text size of the conversation.
- **Minimize** keeps the conversation; **Close** hides the panel and keeps
  the conversation too. **New conversation** starts over.
- **Delete conversation** removes it from the King Jahmir server after you
  confirm. **Delete all** removes every conversation you have had with him.
- Links in replies open app windows (they appear as "Open: <window>") or
  help pages on the King Jahmir server. Other web links are shown as text and
  never opened automatically.
- Every status line has a symbol and a word (for example "✓ Sent",
  "⚠ Unavailable"), so it reads without colour.
- Keyboard: **Tab** moves between controls, **Enter** sends, **Esc** closes
  the panel.

## Privacy settings in the panel

- **Use this screen for help** (off by default): sends the name of the window
  you have in front and the feature it belongs to with your next message,
  so you can ask "Explain this screen". Nothing else about the screen is sent.
- **Live account help** (off by default): lets him read your own account
  records when a records service exists. None exists yet, so account tools
  answer UNAVAILABLE.
- **Remember my preferences** (off by default): lets him propose saving a
  preference, such as "short answers". He saves nothing until you click
  **Confirm**. **Preferences** lists what is saved; you can edit or delete
  each one.
- He never reads your private chats, inventory contents, or other residents'
  information.

## Retention

Conversations are kept on the King Jahmir server for 30 days by default
(the server owner can change this) and then deleted automatically. Deleting
a conversation removes it at once.

## When he is unavailable

If the server cannot be reached, is busy, or the AI service fails, the panel
says so with the reason and a **Retry** button. Retrying sends the same
message again without making a second copy of it.

## Voice

UNAVAILABLE. The viewer has no speech recognition or read-aloud engine yet.
Text chat works on its own.
