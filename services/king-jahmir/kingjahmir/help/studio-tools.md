# 6|7 Studio tools

Every window here opens from **Me > 6|7 Studio**. They keep their files on
your computer and never spend money or send anything without asking.

- **Outfit Doctor...**: save outfits with each item's attachment point,
  see items that share a point, and wear an outfit. After wearing, it
  checks every item and lists any that didn't come back. It never buys a
  replacement for a missing item.
- **Pose and Photo Studio...**: camera angles and poses for your own
  avatar, with snapshots.
- **Photo Studio Looks...**: colour and lighting presets over the
  Lightbox and a local sky. **Turn off** puts back exactly what the look
  changed.
- **Director Mode...**: saved camera angles, camera paths (straight cuts
  when reduced motion is on), shot lists, and PNG frame export. Video
  export is UNAVAILABLE: the viewer has no video encoder yet.
- **Shopping and Demo Organizer...**: stores with map links, a shopping
  list, and demos you tried, tagged by body, size, style and colour. A
  liked demo is a reminder, never a purchase.
- **Recording Privacy Mode...**: one switch hides name tags, chat
  pop-ups and bubbles, your location and your L$ balance on your screen
  before you record or stream. Turning it off puts back exactly what it
  changed.
- **Settings Backup and Restore...**: saves your changed settings and 6|7
  files to one file, and shows exactly what a restore would change before
  it happens. Passwords, tokens, API keys and stream keys are never
  included.
- **Sound Equalizer...** (also **Me > Preferences > Sound > Equalizer**):
  an on/off switch and 10 bands for world and interface sounds, with
  Flat, Bass Boost, Voice Clarity and Night Listening presets, your own
  presets, reset, and level meters in words. Parcel music and voice chat
  play outside the viewer's mixer, so they can't be equalized; the
  window says so.
